# Data, dependencies and attribution

The included PGNs derive from the official Stockfish Fishtest dataset:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/official-stockfish/fishtest_pgns

Source test: `25-01-01/677492ae1a2f267f20548afc`.
The dataset publisher identifies its license as LGPL-3.0. The included copies of
LGPL-3 and GPL-3 accompany the dataset excerpts. Headers and mainline moves are
retained; comments and variations are removed. `data/provenance.json` specifies
the exact archive checksum and transformation. The complete original archive is
available through `scripts/download_source.py`; it is omitted from this package.

The data is from Stockfish development versions playing LTC games on Fishtest.
We make no claim that each move is an exact game-theoretic optimum, or that every
development version has identical playing strength. No AlphaZero data is included.

Dependencies are installed separately and retain their own licenses:

- python-chess/chess: https://github.com/niklasf/python-chess (GPL-3.0 or later).
- PyTorch: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch (BSD-style license).
- NumPy: https://github.com/numpy/numpy (BSD-3-Clause).
- FastAPI and Uvicorn: their respective upstream licenses.

The frozen earlier model and source in `baselines/` are copied from this project's
previous `Chess_Move_GPT_Prototype.zip` artifact. The new architecture is implemented
in `src/chess_policy/`; the baseline is used only for comparison.

No Stockfish executable, neural weights from Stockfish, GPT-4 weights, proprietary
OpenAI architecture or AlphaZero model weights are bundled.

V4 adds a compact excerpt of source games 3,301–3,700, retaining headers and
mainline moves under the same dataset attribution above. New correction labels
were generated offline by Stockfish 15.1 on learner trajectories; its binary
identity, hash and node budget are recorded in `v4_data/corrections/manifest.json`.
The exact mate datasets are generated by this project's sampler and validated
through python-chess. No AlphaZero games were used.

V5 adds source games 3,701–4,100 under the same Fishtest attribution and transformations.
Its pawnful tactical labels are constructed and exhaustively verified offline by
project code using python-chess. They are not new Stockfish or AlphaZero games.
